# noh8 starts_mid_page: scan check

All 31 items, checked against build/pages/noh8/. Format: key | piece | ref | verdict | what I saw. Refs count systems from 000. noh8 is the Vesperale: a piece is a Vespers office; its antiphons are set with psalm tones, and the chapter, hymn, versicles and prayer are often text lines or references to an earlier page between the music. A piece's first music is therefore often the Magnificat antiphon, with a heading above it (the first antiphon of the office is on the next system, or the whole office is by reference). RIGHT means the heading and the first music sit directly on that system and the system above is the end of the previous piece. RUBRIC ONLY means the piece is only text. The queue's printed page was mapped to the pdf page with the catalogue's page map (offset 30); it agreed with the printed numbers on the scans.
