## Corrections to earlier typeset choices

`al_jubilate_deo.csv.ly` names NOH1 p. 115 and matches GregoBase 802 (mode III) at melody/text 1.0. The earlier c-0026 override assigned it to Advent I; it is removed so the transcription serves `noh1-p112/alleluia`, the scanned Jubilate Deo. The Beatus homo and Exaltabo te editor choices are retained, with their guards updated because the new chant links now let the matcher confirm those same targets.

Rematching adds 8 accompaniment targets to the manifest. Candidate transcriptions of Quia factus es and In Deo salutare remain unmatched to the nearby Ego autem cantabo and Sicut oliva sections: their texts and melodies differ. Te gloriosus has an existing LilyPond syntax error and remains a scan; linking its GABC does not claim its accompaniment is renderable.
