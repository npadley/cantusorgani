## Remaining gaps and source damage

- **Assumption: O prima, Virgo, pródita.** Book VII has no indexed setting of this text. Its older Assumption/Marian material cannot supply a verified replacement. All 14 existing I/II Vespers missing-accompaniment notes remain. No O gloriosa Virginum or Ave maris stella substitution was made.
- **En ut superba criminum.** PDF 101 combines the end of printed page 70 with a cut-off fragment of printed page 72; printed page 71 is missing. The surviving fragment remains reachable in Varia with a source warning, but is excluded from the complete Hymns index and related-setting links. VIII's existing accompaniment remains available.
- **Benedictus (Cant. Zachariae), printed p. 70.** Only its heading survives before the composite seam. It has a source warning and zero music systems; none of the adjoining hymns is attributed to it.
- **Exsurge**, **Benedic anima mea (Psalmus 102)**, and **Parce Domine (et ne des)** have no independent music ranges: references or printed text are retained with explanatory source notes. They are not hymn-index entries.
- Existing missing psalm-tone accompaniments and any unrelated missing office components remain. No available VII match establishes a verified replacement for a missing hymn slot in the current dated lineup, so no VII fallback was invented. New links improve discovery without claiming every day is complete.
